About us

Local jet washing you can trust

BN JetWashes is a Daventry-based team that cleans outdoor surfaces for homes and small businesses. We keep the process simple: you tell us what needs cleaning, we give a free quote, then we get the job done.

Who we are

We started BN JetWashes to help people around Daventry bring tired driveways, patios and decking back to life. Green moss, black spots and slippery algae build up fast in the UK climate. You should not need heavy kit or a whole weekend to fix it.

We turn up ready to work, take care around plants and property, and leave the area tidy. No fuss. Just a cleaner outdoor space you can use and enjoy.

How we work

  1. Get in touch — call, text or email with photos if you have them.
  2. Free quote — we confirm the job and give a clear price.
  3. We clean — careful jet washing suited to the surface.
  4. You enjoy it — safer footing and a brighter look.
